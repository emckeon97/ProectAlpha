# GifScroll

A comedy GIF and meme app for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. Swipe through an endless feed of funny GIFs,
react with a laugh, talk trash in the comments, and upload your own memes for
the community.

## Compatibility

- **iOS** 17 and later (iPhone)
- **iPadOS** 17 and later (iPad, all orientations)
- **macOS** (via Mac Catalyst — runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs)

## What it does

- **Feed** — full-screen, TikTok-style swipe feed of comedy GIFs. The feed leans
  funny by default and learns your taste: reacting to GIFs teaches it what you
  like, so more of that surfaces first.
- **Upload** — community meme feed. Post your own memes for everyone to see.
- **Comments** — every GIF and every meme has its own thread. No account needed.
- **Reactions** — one-tap laugh react on anything.
- **Reports** — flag anything that crosses the line; reports go to moderation.
- **Accounts** — optional sign-in to post under your own name.
